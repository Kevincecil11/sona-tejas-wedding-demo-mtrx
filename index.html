Good to know

Questions

How do we get there?
Mahabalipuram is about an hour from Chennai airport along the East Coast Road. Cab and shuttle details will be shared closer to the date.
Where are guests staying?
Rooms are being held for family and friends nearby. Stay details follow with the formal invitation.
What will the weather be like?
Late February is warm and breezy, around 24 to 31°C. Light fabrics by day and a shawl for the evenings.
Can we bring the kids?
Of course. Little ones are very welcome, and the haldi is even more fun with them around.
What footwear should I pack?
Flats, juttis or kolhapuris. Most of the celebrations are on sand and stone, so leave the stilettos at home.
Is there a gift registry?
Your presence is the gift. If you would still like to bless the couple, a token at the wedding is more than enough.
Is there a wedding hashtag?
#SonaWedsTejas. Share every moment, but please keep phones away during the muhurtham.
